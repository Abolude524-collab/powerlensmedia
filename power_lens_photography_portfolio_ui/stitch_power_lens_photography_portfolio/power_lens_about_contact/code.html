STATEMENT / PHILOSOPHY

BEYOND THE GLASS

Edwards Godspower (@mikano_shot_it) arrow_outward

Edwards Godspower is a documentary and fine-art mobile photographer obsessed with natural contrast, raw street rhythm, and unscripted human emotion.

Utilizing the intimacy and immediacy of mobile lenses, Power Lens redefines modern visual craft—proving that world-class visual storytelling is defined by the eye behind the frame, not the weight of the rig. The ethos rejects bloated crew logistics in favor of stealth, natural presence, and cinematic light mastery directly within pulsing metropolitan atmospheres.
